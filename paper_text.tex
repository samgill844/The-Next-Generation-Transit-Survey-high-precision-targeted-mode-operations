The Next-Generation Transit Survey (\ngts) facility consists of an array of twelve telescopes designed to make the highest-precision ground-based photometric observations of exoplanet transits.  Our focus is finding and characterising objects that are suitable for detailed study with large facilities, particularly those of ESO, which are co-located with NGTS in Chile. The NGTS facility is described in detail by \citet{2018MNRAS.475.4476W}.
